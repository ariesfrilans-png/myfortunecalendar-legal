Privacy Policy — My Fortune Calendar

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information My Fortune Calendar ("the app," "we," "our") collects, how it is used, and how it is stored. We wrote this to describe exactly what the app actually does — not a generic template — so please read it as a factual account of our practices.

1. Summary

My Fortune Calendar computes personal numerology and Vedic astrology (Tarabala) results entirely on your own device. We do not operate a server that stores your personal data, we do not sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers, and the app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software.

2. Information We Collect

To compute your personalized calendar, the app asks you to enter:

  • Name — used for name-based numerology calculations and to label profiles if you use the family/multi-profile feature
  • Date of birth — used for birthdate-based numerology calculations
  • Birth star (Janma Nakshatra) — used for Tarabala calculations

This information is stored only in the app's private storage area on your own device, using Android's standard local storage system (DataStore). It is never transmitted to us, to any server we operate, or to any third party. We have no way to see, access, or retrieve this information ourselves — it exists only on your device.

If you use the notification feature, your choice of which days to be notified about and which profiles to include is also stored locally on your device in the same way.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location. The app does not request location permissions and does not access GPS.
  • We do not require or collect an email address, phone number, or any account credentials to use the app.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and the app displays no advertisements.
  • We do not integrate any third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software.

4. Subscriptions and Payment

My Fortune Calendar offers an optional paid subscription for additional features. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, see, or store your payment card details, billing address, or other financial information — that information is handled directly by Google, according to Google's own Privacy Policy. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a subscription is active, which we use to unlock the relevant features on your device.

5. Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app uses Android's WorkManager system to check your selected profile(s) once daily and, entirely on your device, decide whether that day's result matches your chosen preference. No data about this process is sent anywhere — the check and the resulting notification both happen locally on your phone.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes. Because your data is stored only on your own device and never reaches us, there is nothing for us to share even if we wanted to.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your profile information remains on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. You can:

  • Delete an individual profile at any time from within the app (Profile List screen)
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the app, which permanently removes everything the app stored on your device

Since we never receive a copy of your data, there is nothing stored on any server for us to delete on your behalf — removing it from your device is complete and final.

8. Children's Privacy

My Fortune Calendar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13. The family/multi-profile feature is intended for an adult to manage profiles for family members (which may include their own children) as part of a single household's use of the app — not for a child to independently create or manage their own account.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
