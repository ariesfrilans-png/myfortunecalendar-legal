Racer 89: Bike Traffic Racing — Privacy Policy

Developer: Aries Frilans · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Racer 89: Bike Traffic Racing (package com.ariesfrilans.racer89) uses, and why.

1. No accounts, no personal details

The game has no sign-in and no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. Game progress stays on your phone

Your coins, bikes, upgrades, stars, best scores and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

3. Motion sensor

The game reads your phone's accelerometer only while you play, to steer the bike by tilting. These readings are used on the device and are never stored or sent anywhere.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, limited to content suitable for all ages (rating G). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system version and language);
  • ad interaction data (such as which ads were shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic information.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is sent over encrypted connections. You can read how Google uses it here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: in Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID, or to opt out of personalised ads.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Deleting data: uninstall the game to delete all game data on your device. For advertising data, use the Android settings above or contact Google.

6. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
